The domestic multiple rocket launcher, Chunmoo, will now be exported to Croatia. Hanwha Aerospace is set to sign a Chunmoo export contract with Croatia on the 8th, local time. The contract is worth 435.2 million euros, or about 680 billion won, including 18 rocket launchers and missiles. Croatia currently operates Soviet-made 122mm multiple rocket launchers. This equipment has been in service for over 30 years and has a range of about 20km. By introducing Chunmoo, they are expected to operate various guided missiles with ranges from 30km up to 300km. In particular, Chunmoo is a vehicle-mounted mobile multiple rocket launcher system that can move quickly and fire in a short time. Croatia's choice of Chunmoo is also driven by security concerns in Europe following the Russia-Ukraine war. Croatia is accelerating military modernization by significantly increasing defense spending, and this year, it even reinstated conscription after 18 years. If the contract is signed as planned, Croatia will be the fourth European nation to operate Chunmoo, following Poland, Estonia, and Norway.
