What was the driving force that sublimated the fatal disadvantage of explosion into the safest possible advantage? The disposable butane gas canisters we easily buy at the store for a few dollars are packed with state-of-the-art safety engineering that is hard to believe. The principle behind a gas canister explosion is surprisingly simple. When heat from a grill is transferred to the gas can, the liquid gas inside vaporizes rapidly , increasing in volume; the steel container, unable to withstand the pressure, tears open, causing the gas inside to burst out all at once. Korean manufacturers began to think of ways to fundamentally block this catastrophic chain reaction. That is how the explosion-proof valve technology was born. It is designed so that when the internal pressure of the canister rises abnormally to a critical point, a tiny, thin slit at the top of the container gives way and opens first. Simply put, instead of the canister tearing apart in all directions like a balloon, it releases gas little by little through a safe passage, like a steam vent on a pressure cooker, gradually extinguishing the flame. Butane gas from Korea, equipped with this amazing technology, will never explode even if thrown into a fire of several hundred degrees. As the internal pressure rises , the rim joint of the gas can expands, opening a vent through which gas leaks out and simply burns upward like a candle. Foreign buyers who witnessed this demonstration for the first time could not close their mouths at the sight unfolding before them. Seeing it gently burn off the gas instead of exploding with a loud bang and sending metal flying everywhere—it is no wonder they were so shocked.
