For the first time, a Korean player more expensive than Son Heung-min had emerged. Before we continue, let's divide the term "market value" into two . One is the transfer fee. It is the money a club actually pays to the original club to bring in a player. The other is market value. It is the estimated price set by sites like Transfermarkt on how much it would cost to buy the player now. In stock terms, the transfer fee is the price I paid, and the market value is the current share price. The purchase price remains the same once recorded, but the share price fluctuates every day. And the share price doesn't move based on performance alone. The stories, reputation, and trust surrounding the player are all reflected. Please keep this in mind. The 50 million euros Bayern Munich paid for Kim Min-jae surpassed Son Heung-min’s 30 million euros, which was the highest amount ever for an Asian player, let alone a Korean. In terms of market value alone, Kim Min-jae was already a player who had surpassed Son Heung-min three years ago. But in terms of reputation, it is the exact opposite. One person remains the standard for the national team even after moving to the US, while the other is told to leave the national team even while playing for the world’s top club. Isn't that strange? The reputation of a player who moved from a top European league to the US remains the same, while the reputation of a player starting for the world's best club is at rock bottom. This is the answer I told you about at the beginning. In Korea, a player’s reputation is not determined by their league. Their market value doesn't determine it either. It is determined by the trust built within the national team. Now, let’s connect the past two or three years in a single line. At the 2018 Asian Games, Son Heung-min and the '96-line 'win a gold medal together. That same September, Son Heung-min becomes the official captain. In 2022, the masked captain in Qatar creates the winning goal for Hwang Hee-chan, leading the team to the Round of 16. In 2023, Kim Min-jae goes to Bayern Munich with the highest market value for an Asian player. Mentality comments on social media, unfollowing, and for the first time, rumors of cliques circulate . In 2024, there was friction at the Asian Cup, and Son Heung-min shielded his juniors. That spring, Kim Min-jae was blamed as the culprit in the match against Real, and his market value began to drop. In 2025, Son Heung-min left for the US, and Kim Min-jae was pushed down to the third-choice center-back at Bayern. In the summer of 2026, the World Cup ended with 1 win and 2 losses, the coach resigned, and in the fall, there was the tears-filled record-breaking night for Son Heung-min , followed by retirement rumors, bullying allegations, and even a parliamentary audit; as you can see, the collapse didn't happen overnight. Scenes piled up one by one over three years collapsed all at once due to a single rumor. Then, who receives the bill for this collapse? The first bill goes to the players themselves. Reputation moves separately from market value, but it eventually comes back to impact endorsements, support, and future contracts. There are no public figures for a player's personal advertising income, so it cannot be precisely calculated. However, the direction is clear. It is not easy for a Korean advertiser to use a player as a model when their club’s social media account is flooded with "get out" comments. The second bill is paid by the national team. There is no coach, the middle generation is shaken, and the captain is at the center of rumors. If the middle generation is shaken, who should the younger players look up to and learn from? As Lee Chun-soo said, there is even worry that in this state, no matter who you bring in as coach, it might not work. The third bill is received by the association. The principle that what happens in the locker room should be solved in the locker room is fine. But while that locker room door was kept shut, deepfake videos were circulating outside. Unexplained gaps are always filled with the most sensational stories. And the last bill is paid by our citizens. Actual fan comments included words like these. Just work hard at your club. Don't come to the national team. I don't want to see emotional, old-fashioned lineups in the national team anymore. It is natural to be angry. But the moment this anger is fueled by unverified rumors, the reasons to watch the national team disappear one by one. That comment you saw earlier, "If it weren't for Son Heung-min, I wouldn't even watch national team soccer." What makes this scary is that the day Son Heung-min leaves will surely come one day. So, there is something I really want you to take away from today's story. First, check the source of a rumor. Even a video with 1.69 million views is just a rumor if it lacks a single line of recording. It’s not too late to save your anger for confirmed facts. Second, reputation is tarnished by words faster than by skill. Kim Min-jae is a starter for his team again now. Yet, that one remark from three years ago still follows him to this day. It is the same in our workplaces. Performance can recover, but once words are spoken, recovery is much slower. Stocks are the same. Earnings can bounce back in a quarter, but trust, once broken, takes years to restore. Third, a leader's vacant spot will surely be filled by someone else. If it’s not filled by a person, rumors will fill that void. Whether it’s a national team, a company, or a home, if you leave a vacancy for too long, the most sensational stories will take its place .
