In September, our nation's exports exceeded $ 120 billion for the first time in history. Cumulative exports from January to September of this year also surpassed $ 800 billion for the first time. According to the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy, last month's exports reached $ 120.94 billion, an increase of 83.5%from the same month last year. Imports stood at $ 71.09 billion. The trade balance recorded a surplus of $ 49.85 billion, exceeding $ 40 billion for the first time ever. Cumulative exports from January to September hit $ 814.5 billion, already surpassing last year's annual export total of $ 793 billion. Average daily exports also reached $ 5.63 billion, topping $ 5 billion for the first time. As expected, semiconductors led the export record. September semiconductor exports reached $ 60.3 billion, a massive surge of 262.8%compared to the same period last year. This breaks the record for the highest monthly performance set just last month. This is due to increased memory demand centered on AI data centers, with both export volume and prices rising. Other items also contributed to the growth. Computer exports rose by over 435%, driven by demand for AI infrastructure. Cosmetics also recorded their highest performance ever. Ship exports also increased by about 30%as deliveries of LNG carriers rose. By region, exports to China, the U.S., and ASEAN saw significant growth. The government expects that if this trend continues, reaching $ 1 trillion in annual exports is possible. However, with global protectionism and tensions in the Middle East remaining as variables, the key is whether the current export growth, led by semiconductors, can be sustained in the fourth quarter.
